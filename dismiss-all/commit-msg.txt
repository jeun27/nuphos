feat(desktop): dismiss every question shown in the Inbox at once

The Inbox had no way to clear itself: each question had to be dismissed
on its own. "Dismiss all" in the Inbox page's header, beside its search
and count, dismisses every question it shows (not snoozed ones, and only
those a search matches). It shows when there are two or more, asks first
with the standard confirmation, and goes through the same 5 s Undo hold
as a single Dismiss: they all leave at once, one Undo brings them all
back unsent, and once the window passes each is dismissed through the
existing resolve route, so each Bot or Task that asked is told. Those
refused come back in the list, and the toast says how many.

- useInbox: holdAnswer is built on a new holdReplies, which holds any
  set of questions under the one Undo window.
- dismissAll.ts: holdDismissals, and the result of a partial failure.
- inboxItems: the Inbox's search filter moves to shownQuestions.
- DismissAllButton: Button and ConfirmDialog in the toolbar's right
  slot; the Inbox's keys rest while it asks.
- Five new strings in all 7 locales.
- e2e: two Tasks ask, Undo keeps both open, then both close and both
  are told. Registered in questionScenarios.
