Workspace invites can't pick a role, and a pending invite can't be resent

Today every workspace invitation joins as Editor. An admin who wants a Viewer or another Admin has to wait until the person joins and then change their role. A pending invite also has only one action, Cancel invite. If the email never arrived, the admin can't send it again or copy a link to send another way. The only way out is to cancel and invite again. When no email goes out at all (email not set up, or the send failed), the app still says "Invitation sent".

Expected:
- The admin picks what the person joins as (Editor, Viewer or Admin) when inviting. A pending invite shows that role.
- An Admin invitation only makes an Admin while whoever sent it is still an admin of the team. Otherwise the person joins as Editor.
- Admins can resend a pending invitation's email, at most every few minutes, and can copy its invite link.
- When no email went out, the app says so and tells the admin what to do next.
