fix(backend): keep a member's computer streams open on a role change

A role change called revokeTeamBoxStreams, a leftover from before #1391 when an
administrator managed every team computer by role. Since #1391 deviceAccess grants
only by owner and access list, so a role change grants or removes nothing on a
computer. Only leaving or being removed closes streams (revokeTeamMemberAccess).
