Changing a member's role closes their open sessions on the team's cloud computers

When an administrator changes a member's role to Editor or Viewer (from any role, including Editor to Viewer), every terminal, computer-use and chat stream that member has open on the team's cloud computers (Boxes) is closed. Their access to those computers did not change: since #1391 a team role grants nothing on a computer, only the computer's owner and its access list do. So the member just reconnects and carries on, after losing what they were doing.

Expected: a role change leaves what the person has open on the team's computers alone. Only leaving the team or being removed from it should close those streams.
