fix: let a computer's managers sign its agents in from chat

Managers of a computer may sign its agents in (#2431), but the Desktop app
told them to ask the owner, and "Ask to sign in" reached only the owner.

- Desktop: signInRoute gives managers the Sign in dialog, also before the
  agent is listed (the computer is read from the runtime id). The managed
  computers come from one shared, cached device list (useManagedComputers).
- Backend: "Ask to sign in" goes to everyone who may sign the agent in,
  owner first, then managers; a manager who asks is told they can sign it
  in themselves (computerSigners, the rule runtime-login.ts enforces).
- e2e: a manager of a teammate's computer starts its agent's sign-in, one
  who only uses it is refused, and their ask reaches owner and manager.
