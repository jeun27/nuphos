Removing someone from a chat leaves them use of the chat's computer

When a chat runs on someone's computer, inviting a teammate who can't use that computer asks "Also give use of this computer? ... Continue". Confirming gives the teammate Use of the whole computer, not just this chat. That means any chat on it, with its files, commands and browser, on the Claude Code or Codex sign-in there. The dialog doesn't name the computer and doesn't say how far this goes.

Removing that teammate from the chat later, or pressing Undo on the invite, takes the chat away but not the computer. Nothing tells the owner that the teammate can still use it. The only way to take it back is the computer's Share dialog, and only if the owner remembers to.

Expected:
- The invite confirm names the computer and says what Use allows.
- After a removal, the chat's owner is offered to stop sharing the computer with that person too.
- Undo on an invite takes back the computer use that the invite gave.
