fix(ios): offer Switch runtime when a conversation's runtime can't take a turn

Runtime errors (not found, disabled, offline, signed out) and an owner's decline were red text with nothing to press, the sign-in instructions pointed at a "Settings → Runtimes" that doesn't exist and told a teammate to sign in on someone else's computer, and the runtime chip vanished when the runtime was gone.

HintRow and ShareApprovalStrip offer the conversation's existing Switch runtime for those cases, the copy says to switch, the instructions name the desktop's Runtimes page and send a teammate to the owner, and the chip falls back to "Choose runtime".
